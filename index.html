About

Li Cai is an Associate Professor of Finance at the Stuart School of Business, Illinois Institute of Technology. Her research spans hedge funds, ETFs and index tracking, socially responsible and factor investing, options, corporate social responsibility, risk management, econometrics, and empirical asset pricing. Her work has appeared in journals including the Journal of Business Ethics, the Journal of Portfolio Management, the Journal of Futures Markets, the Journal of Investing, Financial Review, and etc.

She holds a PhD in Finance from the University of Massachusetts, Amherst, an MS in Financial Mathematics from the University of Warwick in UK, and a BS in Mathematics from Wuhan University in China.
